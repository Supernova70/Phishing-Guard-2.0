Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026  ·  Contact: meetranga2614@gmail.com

1. What this service is

Phishing-Guard 2.0 (“the Service”) analyses the contents of emails in your mailbox to detect phishing, malware, spoofing and other threats, and presents scan results, geolocation and forensic reports in a personal dashboard. It is provided for your own mailbox only.

2. Information we access and collect

  • Google account identity data — your name, email address and profile picture, via Google Sign-In (scopes: openid, email, profile).
  • Gmail message content — read-only access to your email messages and settings via the Gmail API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly, used solely to scan your messages for threats.
  • Derived analysis data — scan results, threat scores, YARA/metadata matches and forensic artefacts generated by the Service from your messages.
  • Technical data — a session cookie (phishing_guard_session), timestamps, and service health logs.
  • Encrypted credentials — Google OAuth refresh tokens, stored encrypted at rest (Fernet/AES) and never exposed to the frontend.

3. How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in.
  • To scan and analyse your emails for phishing and malware, and to display the results in your private dashboard.
  • To generate forensic reports, geolocation views and campaign/alert features that you open yourself.
  • To keep the Service running securely (session handling, abuse prevention, audit logs of sign-in events).

We never send, modify, delete or forward your email. Access is strictly read-only, and each account’s data is isolated to that account.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your mailbox content with other users of the Service.
  • We do not use your email content for advertising or model training.
  • We do not request write, send, or delete permissions on your Gmail account.

5. Sharing with third parties

Suspicious URLs and file hashes found during a scan may be submitted to VirusTotal for reputation lookups, in line with its terms. Full message bodies are never sent to third parties. Other processors we rely on: Google APIs (authentication and Gmail read), Amazon Web Services (hosting and database), all of which are bound by their own data-protection terms.

6. Google API data

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Storage and security

  • Data is stored in an encrypted-at-rest PostgreSQL database on AWS infrastructure.
  • OAuth refresh tokens are additionally encrypted with Fernet (AES-128-CBC + HMAC) before storage.
  • Sessions use an HTTP-only, SameSite cookie; all traffic is served over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access to production systems is restricted to the operator of the Service.

8. Retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account exists. You can at any time: (a) revoke the Service’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops all Gmail access; and (b) request full deletion of your account and all stored messages, scan results and tokens by emailing the contact above. Deletion is completed within 30 days of a verified request.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including GDPR and comparable data-protection regimes), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, a copy of your data in a portable format, and restriction of processing. Sign-in events are recorded for security; you may request their removal as part of deletion.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be announced on the sign-in page.

12. Contact

Privacy questions, data requests or complaints: meetranga2614@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 7 days.
